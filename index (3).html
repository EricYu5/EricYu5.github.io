Bender Trust Equity Research Competition: Top 3 of 64 Teams

As a member of Little Investment Bankers of Rutgers, I worked with a team of five to research Powell Industries (POWL), an electrical equipment manufacturer, and recommend a Buy. Our thesis rested on its record backlog, its acquisition of Remsdaq, and a debt-free balance sheet. We also covered industry competition, key risks and mitigants, an income statement, a revenue build with projections, and a comps-based valuation. Our team placed top 3 out of 64 teams.
